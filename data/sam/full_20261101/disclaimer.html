What this site is

This site publishes the output of statistical models built on publicly available sports data — historical box scores, play-by-play records, team and player statistics, injury reports and posted market numbers. The models estimate likely outcomes. They produce numbers, and those numbers are displayed as they come out.

A projection is an estimate of an average outcome across many hypothetical repetitions of an event that will in fact happen exactly once. A player projected for 24.6 points will not score 24.6 points. The number describes the centre of a distribution, not a prediction of what will occur, and the distribution is wide.

Where a number on this site has been set by hand rather than produced by the model, it is labelled as such on the page it appears on.
